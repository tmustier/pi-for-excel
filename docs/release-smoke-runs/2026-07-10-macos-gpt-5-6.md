## Luna interpretation

The transport split was reproduced deliberately rather than inferred from the UI. The model ID, provider, metadata, credential mapping, and selector behavior are correct. The failing path is specifically the upstream ChatGPT SSE route; the Node WebSocket and API-provider paths accept Luna. Sol remains the default, so this rollout boundary does not put fresh sessions onto an unavailable tier.
